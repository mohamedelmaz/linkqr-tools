About Us

LinkQR Tools is a small collection of free, privacy-first online tools for creating links and QR codes directly in your browser.

We built these tools because we needed simple, fast utilities that do not require accounts, logins, or software installation. Everything runs locally in your browser, so your data stays with you.

Our story

We didn't set out to build another QR website. We set out to build the one we wished existed.

Every tool here runs entirely on your device. That's not a feature — it's a promise.

Free means free: no trials, no watermarks, no upgrade-to-pro surprises.

The internet got complicated. We're fixing it — one simple tool at a time.

Our goal is to add useful tools one by one, keep the interface clean, and avoid heavy dependencies. If you have a tool request, feel free to reach out.
