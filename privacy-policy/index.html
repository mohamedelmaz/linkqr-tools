Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

This Privacy Policy explains how LinkQR Tools handles information when you use our website and tools.

Overview

LinkQR Tools is a collection of browser-based tools. We do not operate a backend server for tool processing. Most tools run entirely in your browser.

Tools processing

Any data you enter into our tools, such as phone numbers or messages, is processed locally in your browser. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

Cookies

We may use essential cookies for basic site functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies unless explicitly stated and enabled by you.

Analytics

If we enable analytics, it will be clearly disclosed here. Analytics may collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the site.

Advertising

We may display ads served by third-party advertising networks. These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect the functionality of some tools.

Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. Since our tools process data locally in your browser and we do not collect personal data on our servers, these rights are inherently protected. If you have concerns about data handling, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@linkqr.tools.
