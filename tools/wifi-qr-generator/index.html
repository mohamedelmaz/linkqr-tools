Frequently asked questions

What is WPA vs WEP vs No Password?
WPA/WPA2 is the most common and secure encryption. WEP is older and less secure. No Password means the network is open.

When should I use the hidden network option?
Select "Yes" if your router does not broadcast its SSID. Most home networks are not hidden.

Why does the QR code not work?
Make sure you selected the correct encryption type. Some older devices do not support WPA3. Also, special characters in passwords may cause issues on some devices.
