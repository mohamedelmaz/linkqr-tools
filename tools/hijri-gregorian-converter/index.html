Frequently Asked Questions

What is Umm al-Qura calendar?

Umm al-Qura is the official Hijri calendar used in Saudi Arabia and many Muslim countries. It is based on astronomical calculations and sighting data.

How accurate is the conversion?

This converter uses the browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat with the islamic-umalqura calendar, which is the most accurate widely available method for civil Hijri dates.

Why do Hijri and Gregorian dates differ?

The Hijri calendar is lunar (354 or 355 days) while the Gregorian calendar is solar (365 or 366 days). This causes the Hijri year to be about 11 days shorter, so dates drift relative to the Gregorian calendar.
