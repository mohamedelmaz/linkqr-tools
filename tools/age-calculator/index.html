Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this age calculator?

The calculator uses exact calendar math, accounting for varying month lengths and leap years, giving you precise years, months, and days.

Does it handle leap years correctly?

Yes. February 29 births are handled correctly, and the total days calculation accounts for leap years in the date range.

What is the reference date used for?

By default, the reference date is today. You can set a past or future date to calculate your age at a specific point in time.
