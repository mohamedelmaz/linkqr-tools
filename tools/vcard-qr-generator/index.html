vCard QR Generator

Generate a QR code for a contact card.

What is a vCard QR code?

A vCard QR code contains contact information. When scanned, the phone prompts to save the contact directly to the address book.

How to use

Fill in the contact details, click Generate, then download the QR code. Add it to business cards, email signatures, or posters.

Frequently asked questions

What fields are included in the vCard?
The QR code includes: name, phone, email, organization, and website URL.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?
Yes. Both iOS and Android support vCard format (VCF). Scanning the QR code will prompt to save the contact.

Where can I use vCard QR codes?
Business cards, email signatures, event badges, conference materials, and storefront displays.
