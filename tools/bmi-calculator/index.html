Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is BMI?

BMI is a general screening tool. It does not distinguish between muscle and fat, so very muscular people may be classified as overweight despite low body fat.

Does BMI account for muscle vs fat?

No. BMI only considers height and weight. Athletes and bodybuilders often have high BMI due to muscle mass. Use BMI as one of several health indicators.

What is a healthy BMI range?

The WHO defines normal weight as BMI 18.5–24.9. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obese. These are general guidelines for adults.
