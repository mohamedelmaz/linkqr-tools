UTM Builder

Build UTM parameters for tracking marketing campaigns.

What are UTM parameters?

UTM parameters are tags added to URLs to track marketing campaign performance in analytics tools like Google Analytics.

Parameters

utm_source identifies the source (e.g. google, facebook). utm_medium identifies the medium (e.g. cpc, email). utm_campaign identifies the specific campaign. utm_term and utm_content are optional for paid search and A/B testing.
